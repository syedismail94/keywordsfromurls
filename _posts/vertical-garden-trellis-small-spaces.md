Vertical gardening is one of the smartest ways to maximize greenery in limited spaces. Whether you have a tiny balcony, a compact backyard, or even just a wall, a vertical garden trellis can transform your area into a lush green paradise.

## 🌿 What is a Vertical Garden Trellis?

A vertical garden trellis is a structure designed to support climbing plants. It helps plants grow upward instead of outward, making it ideal for small spaces like apartments, terraces, and narrow gardens.

---

## 🌱 Benefits of Using a Trellis

- Space saving for small homes  
- Better air circulation  
- Improved sunlight exposure  
- Enhances home aesthetics  
- Easy maintenance

## 🛠️ DIY Vertical Trellis Idea

**Materials Needed:**
- Wooden sticks or bamboo  
- Rope or wire  
- Nails or zip ties  

**Steps:**
1. Arrange sticks in a grid pattern  
2. Tie intersections securely  
3. Fix to wall or pot  
4. Plant climbers at the base  

---

## 💡 Tips for Success

- Ensure proper sunlight  
- Water regularly  
- Trim plants for shape  
- Use strong support
