Indoor air pollution is a common problem caused by chemicals, dust, and poor ventilation. One of the simplest and most natural ways to improve air quality is by adding air-purifying plants to your home or office. These plants not only enhance indoor aesthetics but also help remove toxins and create a healthier environment.

Below are **25 air-purifying plants** that are easy to grow and ideal for indoor spaces.

## Final Thoughts

Air-purifying plants are a natural and affordable way to improve indoor air quality. Whether you place them in your home, office, or workspace, these plants help create a healthier, calmer, and more refreshing environment.
