Summer is the perfect time to grow fresh herbs at home. Many herbs love heat, grow fast, and need very little care. Whether you have a balcony, terrace, or small garden, these herbs will thrive in Indian summers.

---

## 1. Why Grow Herbs in Summer?

Growing herbs in summer has many benefits:

- Fast growth due to sunlight ☀️  
- Saves money on groceries  
- Fresh and chemical-free  
- Perfect for beginners  

---

## 2. Best Herbs for Summer (Top Picks)

### 2.1 Basil (Tulsi)

- Loves heat and sunlight  
- Water daily but avoid overwatering  
- Used in tea, pasta, and herbal remedies  

💰 Cost: ₹20–₹50 (seeds)

---

### 2.2 Mint (Pudina)

- Grows very fast  
- Needs partial shade  
- Best grown from cuttings  

💰 Cost: ₹10–₹30

---

### 2.3 Coriander (Dhaniya)

- Quick harvest (20–25 days)  
- Needs regular watering  
- Perfect for daily cooking  

💰 Cost: ₹20

---

### 2.4 Lemongrass

- Heat tolerant  
- Low maintenance  
- Great for tea and detox drinks  

💰 Cost: ₹30–₹80

---

### 2.5 Curry Leaves (Kadi Patta)

- Needs full sunlight  
- Long-term plant  
- Essential for Indian cooking  

💰 Cost: ₹100–₹300 (plant)

---

### 2.6 Spinach (Palak)

- Grows quickly  
- Needs watering daily  
- Rich in iron  

💰 Cost: ₹20

---

### 2.7 Fenugreek (Methi)

- Very easy for beginners  
- Ready in 2–3 weeks  
- Can grow in trays  

💰 Cost: ₹10–₹20

## 4. Summer Care Tips for Herbs

- Water early morning or evening  
- Use shade net if heat is too high  
- Use organic compost every 10–15 days  
- Avoid overwatering  

---

## 5. Best Containers & Setup

- Use grow bags or plastic pots  
- Ensure drainage holes  
- Soil mix: Garden soil + compost + sand (1:1:1)  

💰 Setup cost: ₹200–₹500

---

## 6. Final Thoughts

Growing herbs in summer is one of the easiest ways to start gardening. With minimal effort, you can enjoy fresh, healthy, and aromatic herbs daily.

Start small, stay consistent, and your garden will grow beautifully 🌿
