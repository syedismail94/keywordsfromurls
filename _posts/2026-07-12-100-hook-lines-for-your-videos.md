If your video doesn't grab attention in the first three seconds, it's over. People are scrolling fast, and your hook line is the only thing standing between "keep watching" and "next video."

The good news? You don't need to be clever every single time. You just need a bank of hooks that work, and you swap in your own topic. That's exactly what this post gives you — 100 ready-to-use hook lines, sorted into 10 easy categories, so you always have one on hand when you sit down to film.

## Why the First 3 Seconds Matter So Much

Most platforms only show a video to more people if it holds attention early. A weak opening line means low watch time, and low watch time means the algorithm stops pushing your video. A strong hook does the opposite — it makes someone pause, and that pause is what earns you the rest of the views.

Think of your hook as a promise. It tells the viewer exactly what they're about to get, and it makes them curious enough to stay for it.

## 1. Curiosity Hooks

These work because the brain hates an unfinished thought. Once you open a "gap" in someone's knowledge, they need to close it.


1. "This is the mistake everyone makes with ___"
2. "Nobody tells you this about ___"
3. "You've been doing ___ wrong your whole life"
4. "Wait for the ending, it's not what you think"
5. "This changed everything about how I do ___"
6. "I found something nobody is talking about"
7. "This looks normal until you see what happens next"
8. "The secret nobody wants to share about ___"
9. "Watch till the end, trust me"
10. "This one detail changes everything"

## 2. Question Hooks

A direct question forces the viewer to answer in their head, and that keeps them locked in.

1. "Have you ever wondered why ___?"
2. "What if I told you ___?"
3. "Why does nobody talk about ___?"
4. "Do you make this mistake every day?"
5. "Ever asked yourself why ___ happens?"
6. "What would you do if ___?"
7. "Is this the reason ___ isn't working for you?"
8. "Can you guess what happens next?"
9. "Why is nobody talking about this?"
10. "What's the one thing holding you back from ___?"

## 3. Shock & Bold Statement Hooks

Use these when you have a strong, slightly surprising claim. They work best when you can actually back them up in the video.


1. "This is going to ruin your morning routine"
2. "Everything you know about ___ is wrong"
3. "I can't believe this is legal"
4. "This is the worst advice I've ever heard"
5. "Stop doing this immediately"
6. "This one habit is destroying your ___"
7. "I lost $1,000 because of this mistake"
8. "This is why you're failing at ___"
9. "You are being lied to about ___"
10. "This will make you rethink everything"

## 4. Story & Personal Hooks

People connect with people, not brands. A personal story hook builds trust fast.

1. "Let me tell you what happened to me last week"
2. "I never thought this would happen to me"
3. "Three years ago, I was completely broke"
4. "This is the story nobody asked for, but you need it"
5. "I almost gave up before this happened"
6. "My biggest failure taught me this"
7. "Here's what changed my life in one day"
8. "I used to think ___, until this happened"
9. "This is my honest experience with ___"
10. "I wasn't going to share this, but here it is"

## 5. Number & List Hooks

Numbers set a clear expectation — the viewer knows exactly how much value is coming and how long they'll wait for it.


1. "5 things I wish I knew before ___"
2. "3 mistakes that are costing you ___"
3. "7 signs you need to ___"
4. "10 things nobody tells you about ___"
5. "The top 3 reasons ___ isn't working"
6. "5 hacks that changed my ___"
7. "3 red flags to watch for in ___"
8. "These 4 tips saved me hours"
9. "6 things every beginner gets wrong"
10. "2 steps to fix ___ instantly"

Speaking directly to the viewer makes the video feel personal, like advice from a friend instead of a broadcast.

1. "You are making this mistake right now"
2. "You need to see this before it's too late"
3. "You've been overpaying for ___"
4. "You're not alone if you struggle with ___"
5. "You deserve to know this"

7. "You can fix this in under 5 minutes"
8. "You've probably never tried this before"

## 7. Problem & Pain Point Hooks

Naming the exact frustration someone feels is one of the fastest ways to earn their attention.


1. "If you're tired of ___, watch this"
2. "Struggling with ___? Here's the fix"
3. "This is why ___ keeps failing"
4. "Tired of wasting money on ___?"
5. "If ___ feels impossible, you're not alone"
6. "Here's why nothing has worked for you yet"
7. "This is the real reason you're stuck"
8. "If you hate ___, this will help"
9. "Frustrated with ___? Try this instead"

## 8. Before & After / Transformation Hooks

These promise a visible, measurable change, which is one of the most-watched formats on every platform.

1. "This is what my ___ looked like before vs after"
2. "From zero to ___ in 30 days"
3. "Here's the transformation nobody expected"
4. "I didn't think this could change this fast"
5. "This is a total glow-up for ___"
6. "Before this, I had no idea what I was doing"
7. "The difference this made is unreal"
8. "This small change had a huge result"
9. "See the before and after for yourself"
10. "This is proof that small changes add up"

## 9. Controversial & Pattern Interrupt Hooks

Use these sparingly, and only when you actually believe your take. They work because they break the pattern of what people expect to hear.


1. "Unpopular opinion: ___ is overrated"
2. "I'm going to say what everyone is thinking"
3. "This might upset some people, but it's true"
4. "Everyone is wrong about ___, here's why"
5. "I don't care if this is controversial"

7. "This goes against everything you've been told"

10. "This is the opposite of what influencers tell you"

## 10. Trend & Pop Culture Hooks

4. "This is why everyone is obsessed with ___"
5. "Jumping on this trend because it's too good"

## How to Choose the Right Hook for Your Platform

Hook length and pacing should match where the video is going. Here's a simple guide.

| Platform | Ideal Hook Length | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| TikTok | 1–2 seconds | Fastest scroll speed, get to the point instantly |
| Instagram Reels | 2–3 seconds | Slightly more visual storytelling works |
| YouTube Shorts | 2–4 seconds | A tiny bit more room to build context |
| YouTube (long-form) | 5–10 seconds | Can include a short story or setup |

## A Simple Framework for Writing Your Own Hooks

Once you're comfortable with these 100, you can start building your own using this basic formula:

1. **Pick an emotion** you want the viewer to feel — curious, validated, surprised, or understood.
2. **Name the topic** in plain, simple words. Don't overcomplicate it.
3. **Add a reason to stay** — a number, a promise, or an unfinished thought.

For example: "3 things (number) I wish I knew about small bedroom lighting (topic) before I wasted $200 (reason to stay)."

Write a few versions of the same hook, say them out loud, and pick the one that feels the most natural coming out of your mouth. A hook that reads well on paper doesn't always sound right when spoken.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**How long should a hook line actually be?**
Aim for one short sentence, ideally under 10 words. If it takes more than 3 seconds to say, it's probably too long.

**Can I reuse the same hook style across videos?**
Yes. Viewers don't remember which exact words you used last time — they respond to the pattern. Rotating through 3–4 favorite styles is completely normal.

**Do hooks need to be spoken, or can they be text on screen?**
Both work, and using them together is even stronger. Say the hook out loud while it also appears as on-screen text for viewers watching without sound.

**What if my hook doesn't match my actual content?**
Avoid this. A hook that overpromises leads to viewers dropping off mid-video, which hurts your future reach. Always make sure the hook is something your video actually delivers on.

## Final Thoughts

A great hook line isn't about being the most creative person on the internet — it's about removing the guesswork. Keep this list saved, pick a category that fits your topic, and swap in your own words. The more you practice saying these out loud, the more natural they'll feel, and the easier it becomes to build a habit of starting strong every single time you hit record.
