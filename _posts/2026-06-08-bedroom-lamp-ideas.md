## Introduction

The right lamp can completely change how your bedroom feels. It's not just about brightness — it's about mood, style, and where you place it.

This guide gives you 10 simple bedroom lamp ideas, explains exactly where to put each one, and shows you what they look like so you can copy the style right away.

---

## 1. Classic Bedside Table Lamp

**Where to put it:** On your nightstand, one on each side of the bed.

A traditional table lamp on your nightstand is the most practical lamp you can add. It gives you soft light for reading without lighting up the whole room.

**Best placement tip:** The bottom of the lampshade should be at shoulder height when you're sitting up in bed. This avoids glare in your eyes.

[![Classic white bedside table lamp on a wooden nightstand]({{ site.baseurl }}/assets/images/home-decor/bedside-table-lamp.jpg)](https://pin.it/1psWFxsOl)

| Feature | Details |
|---|---|
| Best for | Reading, night routines |
| Ideal height | 24–27 inches tall |
| Cost range | $30–$150 |
| Where to buy | IKEA, Target, Amazon, HomeGoods |
| Bulb type | Warm white LED, 2700K |

**Style tip:** Match both lamps if you have a symmetrical bedroom. Mix them if you want an eclectic look.

## 2. Arc Floor Lamp

**Where to put it:** In the corner behind a reading chair, or arching over one side of the bed.

An arc floor lamp is tall with a curved arm that swings the light source over your seating area. It's dramatic, useful, and looks expensive.

**Best placement tip:** Place it behind and slightly to the side of your chair or bed. The arc should reach over your shoulder so light falls on your book or phone — not in your eyes.

[![Black arc floor lamp arching over a cozy bedroom reading chair]({{ site.baseurl }}/assets/images/home-decor/arc-floor-lamp-bedroom.jpg)](https://pin.it/1psWFxsOl)

| Feature | Details |
|---|---|
| Best for | Reading corners, statement lighting |
| Ideal height | 58–72 inches |
| Cost range | $80–$400 |
| Where to buy | West Elm, CB2, Wayfair, Amazon |
| Bulb type | Warm white dimmable LED |

**Style tip:** Black matte or brushed brass arcs are the most popular right now. Match the finish to your bed frame or dresser hardware.

## 3. Pendant Lamp / Hanging Lamp

**Where to put it:** Hang it from the ceiling above each nightstand, instead of a table lamp.

Pendant lamps hung over nightstands are a modern designer move. They free up surface space on your nightstand and give the room a hotel-room feel.

**Best placement tip:** Hang the pendant so the bottom hangs about 18–24 inches above your nightstand surface. Use a hardwire installation or a plug-in pendant cord that you hang on a ceiling hook.

| Feature | Details |
|---|---|
| Best for | Modern/minimal bedrooms, small nightstands |
| Drop length | 18–24 inches above nightstand |
| Cost range | $40–$300 per pendant |
| Where to buy | Rejuvenation, Pottery Barn, Wayfair, Etsy |
| Bulb type | Edison or globe LED, 2200–2700K |

**Style tip:** Fabric cord pendants feel cozy. Metal cage pendants feel industrial. Rattan pendants feel boho.

## 4. Wall Sconce Lamp

**Where to put it:** Mounted directly on the wall on either side of the headboard, about 18–24 inches above the mattress.

Wall sconces are fixed to the wall and save nightstand space completely. They look very polished and are popular in master bedrooms.

**Best placement tip:** Center each sconce above your nightstand position. Install them so the light shines slightly downward for reading, or upward for ambient glow.

[![Warm brass wall sconce mounted beside a bed headboard in a neutral bedroom]({{ site.baseurl }}/assets/images/home-decor/wall-sconce-bedroom.jpg)](https://pin.it/1psWFxsOl)

| Feature | Details |
|---|---|
| Best for | Small rooms, modern/traditional styles |
| Mount height | 60–65 inches from floor |
| Cost range | $50–$350 each |
| Where to buy | Lamps Plus, Shades of Light, Home Depot |
| Bulb type | Warm white LED candelabra |

**Style tip:** Swing-arm sconces are best for reading because you can move the light. Fixed sconces work great for pure ambiance.

## 5. Desk Lamp on the Dresser

**Where to put it:** On top of your dresser or vanity table, in the corner of the room.

Most people forget that a desk lamp on a dresser can act as a secondary light source for your bedroom. It brightens a dark corner and adds visual interest to your dresser display.

**Best placement tip:** Put it at one end of the dresser rather than the center — this creates a natural vignette look. A slim task lamp or a small designer desk lamp both work well.

| Feature | Details |
|---|---|
| Best for | Dressers, vanity tables, dark corners |
| Ideal height | 14–18 inches |
| Cost range | $25–$120 |
| Where to buy | IKEA, Target, Anthropologie, Urban Outfitters |
| Bulb type | Neutral or warm white LED |

**Style tip:** A sculptural lamp (mushroom lamp, dome lamp, or ceramic lamp) on a dresser looks like art.

## 6. Mushroom Lamp (Retro Table Lamp)

**Where to put it:** On a nightstand, side table, or bookshelf in the bedroom.

The mushroom lamp is having a huge moment right now. Its round dome shape gives off a soft, diffused glow and looks stunning in boho, retro, or Scandinavian bedrooms.

**Best placement tip:** Place it on your nightstand if you want it close, or on a low bookshelf for ambient mood lighting across the room.

## 7. LED Strip Lights Behind the Headboard

**Where to put it:** Mounted behind your headboard on the wall, or under the bed frame.

LED strip lights are not just for gaming rooms. When placed behind a headboard, they create a beautiful backlight halo effect — like a sunset glow behind your bed.

**Best placement tip:** Stick the LED strip to the back edge of your headboard so the light glows onto the wall but the strip itself is hidden. Use a warm white or amber color (not blue or cool white — that kills the mood).

[![Warm amber LED strip lights glowing behind a velvet upholstered headboard in a dark cozy bedroom]({{ site.baseurl }}/assets/images/home-decor/led-strip-headboard-bedroom.jpg)](https://pin.it/1psWFxsOl)

| Feature | Details |
|---|---|
| Best for | Accent lighting, Netflix nights, mood |
| Color temp | 2200–2700K warm white or amber |
| Cost range | $15–$60 for the strip |
| Where to buy | Amazon, Best Buy, Home Depot |
| Brand example | Govee, Philips Hue, Kasa |

**Style tip:** Use a smart strip light so you can dim it or change colors from your phone. Govee and Philips Hue both work with Alexa and Google Home.

## 8. Torchiere Floor Lamp

**Where to put it:** In the corner of the bedroom, pointing upward toward the ceiling.

A torchiere is a tall floor lamp with a bowl-shaped shade that points upward. It bounces light off the ceiling, which fills the whole room with soft, even light — no harsh shadows.

**Best placement tip:** Place it in a corner diagonally opposite from your main ceiling light. This balances the lighting and removes dark corners.

| Feature | Details |
|---|---|
| Best for | Overall room brightness, small bedrooms |
| Ideal height | 60–72 inches |
| Cost range | $40–$200 |
| Where to buy | Target, Lamps Plus, Wayfair, IKEA |
| Bulb type | A-shape LED or CFL, 800–1100 lumens |

**Style tip:** Brushed nickel and matte white torchieres blend into any bedroom style without competing with your decor.

## 9. Himalayan Salt Lamp

**Where to put it:** On a nightstand, windowsill, or dresser — anywhere you want a gentle, warm glow.

Salt lamps are carved from natural Himalayan pink salt and give off a soft orange-pink glow. They're not a main light source, but as a mood lamp beside your bed, they're warm and calming.

**Best placement tip:** Put it on your nightstand 1–2 feet from where you sleep. It gives just enough light so you're not fumbling in the dark, without being bright enough to disrupt your sleep.

[![Himalayan salt lamp glowing warm orange on a white marble nightstand in a serene bedroom]({{ site.baseurl }}/assets/images/home-decor/himalayan-salt-lamp-bedroom.jpg)](https://pin.it/1psWFxsOl)

## 10. Clip-On Reading Lamp

**Where to put it:** Clipped directly onto your headboard, bookshelf, or nightstand edge.

A clip-on reading lamp is the most flexible lamp on this list. It attaches anywhere, uses minimal space, and is perfect for people who read in bed without wanting to disturb a partner.

**Best placement tip:** Clip it to the top of your headboard on your side of the bed, or to the top of your nightstand. Point it directly at your book or screen at a downward angle.

| Feature | Details |
|---|---|
| Best for | Reading in bed, small spaces, renters |
| Light type | Directional task light |
| Cost range | $15–$60 |
| Where to buy | Amazon, Target, Container Store |
| Brand example | BenQ, Baseus, TaoTronics |

**Style tip:** Look for one with warm/cool/neutral modes and brightness control. A USB-C rechargeable one means no cord management issues.

## Final Thoughts

The best bedroom lighting uses layers — not just one overhead light. Start with a pair of bedside lamps, add an arc floor lamp in your reading corner, and use LED strips or a salt lamp for mood lighting at night.

You don't have to spend a lot. Most of these ideas cost under $100 and make an immediate difference to how your bedroom looks and feels.

Pick one lamp from this list, try it for a week, and see how different your room feels.

## FAQ

**What is the best type of lamp for a small bedroom?**
Wall sconces or pendant lights are best because they don't take up floor or surface space.

**What color temperature is best for bedroom lamps?**
Warm white — 2700K or lower. Avoid cool white (4000K+) in bedrooms as it disrupts sleep.

**How many lamps should a bedroom have?**
Most designers recommend at least 3 light sources: overhead, bedside, and accent. This gives layered, balanced light.

**Can I use LED bulbs in any lamp?**
Yes. Just check the max wattage on the lamp label. Most LEDs are very low wattage (6–10W) so they fit in almost any fixture.

**Where is the best place to buy bedroom lamps in the USA?**
IKEA, Target, and Amazon for budget. West Elm, Pottery Barn, and Lamps Plus for mid-range. CB2 and Rejuvenation for premium.
